# Dashboard Report


The energy mix of France is slowly evolving, going from a almost only nuclear electicity production to a more diversed one, with stil a majority of nuclear but also with Solar, Hydraulic and Wind energy.


The reason for this is obvious : **Climate change**

But let's take a closer look to it and try to see more precisely what's happening and maybe imagine what will happen.


### The main issue

Depending on the period of the year, the energy mix cannot be the same.
**Why** is it the case ? Because the conditions are changing from summer to winter.
During the summer, it is hot. France produces less with nuclear due to the heat, but more with solar thanks to the illumination.
During the winter, it is cold. Nuclear production increases, but solar decreases.

But there is a **HUGE** problem : winters are getting warmer and shorter.

So the period of the year where France's top 1 electricity production method works efficiently is disappearing. So other sources are needed. But if fossile energies are used and CO2 emmissions increase, this will only make it worse.
So France has to use renewable sources of energies. This is why it is developping soalr and wind sources, as well as hydrolic.


### France situation compared to its neighbors

France has always been an exportating country in term of energy. That is, overall, it exports more energy than it imports. It is one of the most developped country for nuclear energy production and can afford to export a lot of energy.

In 2022, nuclear plants stopped producing for a moment, leaving France in a position where it was forced to import a lot of energy from other countries.

It shows first of all the importance of nuclear production, for France and its neighbors. It can export thanks to nuclear, and other countries benefit from it.
But this also shows the risk of being too dependent on only one type of energy. If all of a suden, all nuclear plants are not able to produce energy, France will lose around 80% of its electricity production and will have to massively import.

On one hand, we could say that nuclear is the future because it produces a lot of energy for very few carbon emmissions. But on the other hand, if we consider pessimistic, or even some realistic predictions for the future years, winters will get warm and summers will get burning.
If nuclear plants are taken down by the heat of the summer, even for 3 days, the consequences would be very harsh. So imagine a heatwave that lasts for 2 entire weeks with temperatures around 40 degrees.



### Conclusion

It is very difficult to estimate what is right to do, and energy crisis, related to wars or climate events could happen at any time.
